    Summary sheets

    One sheet per chapter, to print or annotate. They do not replace the online course; they set down its essentials on something you keep. Activities, audio capsules and interactive diagrams are not included: the full course book carries them, on paper, with their QR codes.

    🔄

    Generated documents

    These PDFs are produced by tools/generate_polycopies.py from tools/polycopies_content.json. Do not fix them in a PDF editor: the fix would be lost on the next run. Edit the content file, then run the script again.

    The sheets

    Why PDF only

    The generator also produces Word files, which stay in assets/polycopies/ but are not offered here. A distributed office document gets edited, copied, and then circulates in diverging versions with nobody knowing which one is authoritative. PDF fixes what should be fixed; the Word file is for you, to produce the next version.
