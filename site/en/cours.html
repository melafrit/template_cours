    Course contents

    The path is made of three chapters, to follow in order or to pick from as you need. By the end, you will know how to design a complete online course, and you will have seen, along the way, every feature of the template.

    🧭

    How to progress

    Each chapter lasts about 30 minutes (reading + activities) and ends with flashcards. The sidebar progress bar tracks how far you've gone on this device.

    Start chapter 1 →

    The chapters

    🎯 1 · Scoping your course

    Define the audience, measurable learning objectives and the through-line before writing a single page.

    Open chapter 1 →

    🧱 2 · Structuring the content

    Break content into modules and sequences, manage cognitive load and build a readable progression.

    Open chapter 2 →

    🎬 3 · Multimedia resources

    Choose the right medium (podcast, slides, infographic, video) and produce it without overspending or scattering.

    Open chapter 3 →

    📝 4 · Assessing learners

    Distinguish formative from summative assessment, write good quiz items and give useful feedback.

    ♿ 5 · Accessibility & delivery

    Make the course accessible to everyone, then publish and deliver it under good conditions.
