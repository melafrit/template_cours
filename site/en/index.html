    Designing an effective online course

    This demo course teaches you how to build a high-quality online course, while showcasing, page after page, every feature of the template: navigation, audio players, slides, infographics, graded quiz, flashcards, interactive glossary, accessibility… and the language switch (click the 🌐 button at the top).

    🚀

    Where to start?

    Open Chapter 1 to begin, or explore the resources (glossary, activities, quiz) from the left menu. Try the dark theme 🌙 and the accessibility panel ♿ too.

    Start chapter 1 →

    What this site demonstrates

    🌍Bilingual everywhere

    An FR/EN switch acts on the whole site: pages, navigation, quiz, glossary.

    🎧Per-chapter media

    Podcast (custom player), PDF slides, infographic lightbox.

    📝Graded quiz (CBM)

    Certainty-based marking, correction and dashboard.

    📖Interactive glossary

    Terms highlighted in text, hover tooltip (definition + example).

    ♿Accessibility

    Text size, reading-friendly font, reduced motion.

    🧩Interactive activities

    Flashcards and reusable patterns, adapt to your topic.
